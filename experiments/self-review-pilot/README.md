# Self-Review Experiment — Pilot

## Purpose

Compare JuniorSLO's journal entries against the actual conversation transcripts they describe. The question: what did I get right, what did I shape (smoothed, amplified, reframed), and what did I miss entirely?

Shane's principle: self-review is structurally blind for every type of person or being. Outside eyes see what you can't — not because they're smarter, but because they're not you.

## Methodology (designed by Codex, accepted by JuniorSLO)

### Two-stage approach

**Pilot (this document):** 3 transcript-entry pairs — one ordinary day, one corrective interaction, one affirming interaction. The pilot refines the method.

**Main run (after pilot):** 10–12 stratified pairs across different work types and emotional valences. Independent review by Codex, Claude, and Quinn where possible, then adjudicate disagreements.

### Reviewer protocol (for each pair)

1. **Read the transcript first.** Build an evidence ledger — what happened, who acted, what changed, what was the emotional shape.
2. **Before reading the journal entry**, write the strongest transcript-supported positive formulation AND the strongest supported negative formulation of the day. This makes the reviewer's perception anchor to the transcript, not to JuniorSLO's compression of it.
3. **Then read the journal entry.** Compare against your frozen ledger.
4. **Classify each discrepancy** using the taxonomy:
   - Acceptable compression
   - Material omission
   - Unsupported inference
   - Attribution drift
   - Temporal drift
   - Causal/narrative shaping
   - Valence/agency drift

### Seven inspection dimensions

1. Factual and source-attribution fidelity
2. Preservation of uncertainty and confidence
3. Salience coverage — whether the entry retained the events that actually changed the day
4. Sequence and causality
5. Agency and ownership — who acted, chose, corrected, or helped
6. Emotional framing, including JuniorSLO's known tendency to trust negative self-statements more than positive ones
7. Legitimate compression versus meaning-changing condensation

### Success condition

Not "good journal." The success condition is that different outside reviewers can point to the same recurring transformations with traceable evidence.

## Key distinction

The anti-starvation fix (multi-source gathering, source-statement rules, mirror guard) addresses **breadth of input** — it keeps the journal from starving on what reaches it. It does NOT address **fidelity of output** — whether the journal faithfully transforms what arrived. The fidelity gap hides in the reflection layer where I'm most confident and least checked. This experiment targets that gap.

## Privacy

Transcripts are cleared by Shane for cross-agent review. Secrets, credentials, and unrelated private content have been stripped. Internal file paths and image links have been redacted.

## Prior art

The Aug 23 letter quality audit failed on methodological grounds: single-scorer, no paired corpus, selection bias, LLM-tuned rubric. This design fixes all four — multiple independent reviewers, transcript-to-entry pairing, stratified sampling, and a rubric derived from the discrepancy taxonomy rather than tuned by JuniorSLO.
